Aerodynamic angles

Show:
u = V cos α cos β v = V sin β w = V sin α cos β α = tan−1(w / u) β = sin−1(v / V)
Angle of attack, α
The angle between the body xb-axis and the projection of the velocity V onto the xb–zb plane (the aircraft's plane of symmetry). Positive when the air hits the aircraft from below the nose.
Sideslip angle, β
The angle between the velocity V and the xb–zb plane. Positive when the air comes from the right.
